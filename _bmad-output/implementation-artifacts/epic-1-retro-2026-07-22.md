# Epic 1 Retrospective: Prove a Trusted Regional Job Platform

**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Epic status:** Complete
**Stories:** 15/15 done
**Participants:** Srudyka (Project Lead), Amelia (Developer), Alice (Product Owner), Winston (Architect), Dana (QA Engineer)

## Epic Review

Epic 1 established the platform Cell foundation and its safety boundaries: canonical contracts, Cell registration and discovery, a platform-owned canary, evidence normalization, expected-occurrence materialization, deterministic occurrence state, ECS launch and completion correlation, deadline detection, alert delivery, Cell health, operator commands, recovery, and lifecycle cleanup.

The strongest outcome was the progressive hardening loop. Initial implementations were followed by adversarial review, edge-case analysis, acceptance auditing, targeted patches, and a full validation rerun. The final Story 1.15 gate passed with 177 tests, 209 contract subtests, strict Ruff/mypy checks, Terraform validation, Checkov with 689 platform and 98 canary checks, and repository hygiene checks.

## What Went Well

- Contract-first development created stable vocabulary for identity, evidence, ownership, recovery, and lifecycle behavior.
- Single-writer and conditional-write patterns consistently protected occurrence and operational state.
- Review feedback found concrete IAM, pagination, lease, metric-producer, rollback, and durability gaps before completion.
- Credential-free validation remained reproducible and clearly separated from live AWS qualification.
- Runbooks and rollback limitations were added alongside infrastructure and runtime behavior.

## Challenges and Lessons

- A green initial validation pass did not guarantee complete acceptance coverage. Adversarial review repeatedly exposed missing authority edges and operational controls.
- “Contract exists” is insufficient without an authoritative producer, a consumer-path test, scoped IAM, metrics, and rollback guidance.
- Pagination, retries, duplicate delivery, and partial failure need scenario fixtures early, not only after review findings.
- Story completion notes must distinguish credential-free proof from live AWS behavior and rollout qualification.

## Review-Hardening Process Decision

For Epic 2, adversarial code review, edge-case review, and acceptance auditing are part of the Definition of Done for every story. Review findings are delivery feedback and must be resolved or explicitly documented before a story is marked done. Validation must be rerun after each remediation cycle.

## Epic 2 Preparation

Epic 2, “Run a Secure Scheduled Job in Non-Production,” depends on the completed Cell Contract, reservation/discovery foundation, occurrence path, alert path, operator controls, and recovery boundaries from Epic 1.

Story 2.1 should make the following explicit before implementation begins:

- authoritative reservation state and collision/transfer rules;
- immutable repository, account, Region, Environment, and ownership binding;
- protected metadata and output boundaries;
- IAM, networking, CONFIG, and acknowledgement dependencies;
- negative scenarios for stale, duplicate, conflicting, and unauthorized declarations.
